# From China to the World: MatrixOrigin Joins the 2026 Google for Startups Accelerator China

MatrixOrigin has been selected for the 2026 Google for Startups Accelerator China.

After several months of applications, screening, and interviews, MatrixOrigin was chosen as one of 10 companies from several hundred applicants this year.

Over the past five years, 45 Chinese startups have taken part in the program. We are glad to be joining that group this year.

[Read Google’s official announcement](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/Ccn9CCr-LGp-9Ki4f_VrMw)

Over the past few years, we have worked on a range of complex enterprise AI projects. One thing has become increasingly clear: once companies start putting AI into real production, many of the underlying problems look surprisingly similar across markets.

How does enterprise data get into AI? How do Agents connect to production systems? How should Context, Memory, and Runtime be organized? How do permissions and governance work in practice? These questions do not disappear when the country or industry changes.

That is why we want to test our products in more markets.

Chinese enterprise environments are complex and demanding, and the engineering experience we have built here matters to us. The next step is to bring that experience to more customers and partners overseas, while continuing to improve the products through exposure to different markets and IT environments.

Joining the Google for Startups Accelerator China also gives us a chance to meet more customers, developers, founders, and partners around the world.

There is still a long road ahead. For now, the priority is simple: keep improving the product and keep solving real problems.

We are glad to be starting this next step alongside nine other companies.

Build in China. Build for the World.
